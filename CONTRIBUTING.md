# Contributing

## How you get paid

Put your wallet address anywhere in your pull request description. That is the
whole process. No signup, no account, no wallet connect.

```
Payout: 0xYourAddressHere
```

When your pull request merges, the Harbormaster reads it, scores the work
against this project's rules, and adds you to the week's payout list with a
written reason you can argue with.

## Two things to know

**Your first address is permanent.** The first wallet an account uses is locked
to it for good. A later edit cannot move where your pay goes, which is what
stops anyone with write access rewriting your address to theirs.

**Check the checksum.** Paste the mixed-case form your wallet gives you. An
address with one wrong character is refused rather than paid, because there is
no way to get those funds back.

## What scores well

Judged on what the change does for the project, not how many lines it touches.
A two-line fix to a real bug outranks a thousand lines of generated boilerplate.
Typos, version bumps and formatting count for very little.

## Seeing your score

Every score appears on the public record with the reason behind it and which
model produced it. Scores can be disputed while the week's clock runs.
